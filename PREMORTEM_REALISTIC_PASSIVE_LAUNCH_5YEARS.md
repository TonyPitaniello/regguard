## 🚨 THE CRITICAL BARRIERS & FAILURE MODES

### Barrier 1: First Channel Doesn't Work (60% risk)

**What could go wrong:**
- IC consultants don't respond to outreach (70% probability in Month 1-3)
- They view RegGuard as competitive threat (20% probability)
- They don't see ROI (existing tools work fine)
- Closing takes 6+ months instead of 3 months

**Mitigation:**
- Don't wait for IC consultants
- Run parallel: IC consultants + sponsors + content
- Get first customer FAST (even 1 paying customer validates model)
- Use first customer as proof for next 10 customers

**Contingency timeline:**
- Month 1-3: No revenue (expect this)
- Month 4-6: First customer acquired (ANY channel)
- Month 7-12: 3-5 customers (proof of concept)
- If no customers by Month 6: Pivot or add new channels

---

### Barrier 2: Churn & Retention Issues (40% risk)

**What could go wrong:**
- IC consultants try RegGuard, then don't renew (60% probability)
  - Reason: Built their own, or switched to ChatGPT, or moved on
- Sponsors test, then disappear (40% probability)
  - Reason: No ROI demonstrated, budget cut, tried cheaper solution

**Mitigation:**
- Monthly check-ins with IC consultants (understand their use)
- Quarterly business reviews with sponsors (show ROI metrics)
- NPS tracking (if NPS < 30, something's wrong)
- Build partnerships (not one-off transactions)

**Realistic retention:**
- Year 1: 60-70% (high churn, first cohort experimental)
- Year 2: 75-85% (improving, relationships deepen)
- Year 3+: 85-95% (network effects, hard to leave)

---

### Barrier 3: Competition & Copying (50% risk)

**What could go wrong:**
- Law firms notice RegGuard and build competitors (10% probability)
- ChatGPT + prompt engineering makes RegGuard free (20% probability)
- Other startups copy the model faster (30% probability)

**Mitigation:**
- Brand & authority (hard to copy - articles, community, expertise)
- Data & history (more projects = better insights)
- Network effects (more IC consultants = more valuable)
- Speed (get to market, build moat BEFORE competitors arrive)

**Reality:**
- RegGuard can be copied in 2-4 weeks by determined competitor
- BUT: Community, brand, relationships take 2-3 years to build
- Defensibility comes from partnerships, not technology

---

### Barrier 4: Founder Burnout (60% risk)

**What could go wrong:**
- Year 1: 20+ hours/week of founder time is BRUTAL
- Response times: 20-30 hours/week of calls, emails, support
- No time for vacation, personal life, other opportunities
- Burnout by Month 8-12 is common

**Mitigation:**
- Hire VA by Month 6 ($500-800/month) to handle support
- Set strict boundaries (no emails after 6pm, no weekends)
- Batch work (one "sales day" per week, not daily)
- Track time & adjust if exceeding 20 hours/week

**Realistic timeline:**
- Months 1-6: Expect 25-30 hours/week (intense but time-bounded)
- Months 7-12: 15-20 hours/week (getting easier)
- Year 2: 15-25 hours/week (scaling phase)
- Year 3: 8-12 hours/week (semi-passive achieved)
- Year 5: 5-7 hours/week (truly passive)
