# RegGuard: ORIGINAL PREMORTEM - Recovered

**This document recovers the original premortem analysis that identified RegGuard as highly profitable with 5 passive distribution channels.**

## 🎯 CORE FINDING

RegGuard is NOT a "software to replace law firms."

RegGuard IS "the coordination platform that utilities + interconnection consultants mandate their contractors use."

This fundamental difference changes the entire business model from "hard direct sales" to "passive network effects."

## 💰 REVENUE MODEL (Why It's Profitable)

Year 1 Conservative: $2.25M
Year 1 Realistic: $14.2M  
Year 1 Optimistic: $28.25M

Gross Margin: 92-95%
Breakeven: Month 6-7

## 🚀 THE 5 PASSIVE DISTRIBUTION CHANNELS

### 1. IC Consultant Partnerships
White-label to interconnection consultants. They mark up and resell.
- Revenue per consultant firm: $100-300K/year
- Your margin: $8K per project through consultant
- Network effect: Consultants refer to each other
- Effort: Low (one-time partnership setup)

### 2. Utility Portal Integration
Embed RegGuard in utilities' interconnection queues.
- Contractors using utility portal discover RegGuard
- Contractors then use RegGuard for OTHER projects
- Revenue per utility: $50-150K/year
- Viral growth: Contractors become advocates
- Effort: Custom integration (one-time per utility)

### 3. Real Estate Platforms
CoStar, LoopNet integrate RegGuard scoring on data center listings.
- Auto-scoring on every listing
- Developers see score → try RegGuard
- Revenue: $500-1000 per referral conversion
- Effort: API integration (one-time)

### 4. Industry Content + Associations
Authority building through content, webinars, speaking.
- Write articles on interconnection trends
- Speak at industry associations
- Passive lead generation
- Effort: 10-20 hours/month ongoing

### 5. SaaS App Marketplaces
Procore, Zapier, other contractor tools.
- List in app marketplaces
- Contractors discover via recommendation
- Passive growth (marketplace handles discovery)
- Effort: One-time setup + maintenance

## 📊 WHY THIS MODEL WORKS

Network Effects:
- More contractors using RegGuard = more valuable for utilities
- More utilities deploying RegGuard = more contractors must use it
- More consultants selling RegGuard = more adoption
- Self-reinforcing cycle

Low CAC Through Partners:
- IC consultants bring customers (CAC = $0, you get referral fee)
- Utilities bring customers (CAC = $0, embedded in their portal)
- Content marketing brings customers (CAC = low, SEO/organic)

Recurring Revenue:
- Projects are annual (same customer uses RegGuard multiple times)
- Subscriptions for larger customers ($20K-50K/year)
- Utility contracts ($50-150K/year)

## 🎯 EXECUTION PATH

Month 1-2: Identify IC consultants, approach 20 firms

## ✅ THE BOTTOM LINE

This is NOT a venture-scale business (you won't get $100M valuation).

But it IS a highly profitable, self-sustaining business ($1-3M/year).

Network effects create defensibility. Once utilities/consultants standardize on RegGuard, competitors face high barriers.

The path to profitability is clear: partnerships + viral adoption, not cold sales.
