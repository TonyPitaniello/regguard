## 🚨 CRITICAL SUCCESS FACTORS (What Could Go Wrong)

### Risk #1: IC Consultants Don't Respond (40% probability)

**Why it happens:**
- Email response rates are notoriously low in B2B
- IC consultants are busy (consulting is delivery-heavy)
- Your email looks like spam

**Mitigation:**
- Personalize every email (mention their recent project, their firm size)
- Use phone calls after 0-2 email responses
- Get warm intro from someone in the industry
- Post on LinkedIn + tag specific people

**Contingency:**
- If email response < 5% by Day 3: Switch to phone calling
- If phone response < 20%: Problem is messaging, not channel
- Solution: Revise pitch based on feedback

---

### Risk #2: They Like RegGuard But Won't Pay (50% probability)

**Why it happens:**
- They're risk-averse (consulting is conservative)
- They want to see long-term case studies
- They think they can build it themselves
- They need internal approval (partner, CFO)

**Mitigation:**
- Offer free 90-day trial (removes risk)
- Get testimonial from first paying customer (social proof)
- Show ROI in dollars, not hours
- Offer "money-back if you don't use it" guarantee

**Contingency:**
- If conversion stalls at 20%: Offer pilot program
- "We'll track your time saved for 30 days, you pay only if ROI is real"
- This removes objection and generates proof

---

### Risk #3: Your Product Isn't Ready (30% probability)

**Why it happens:**
- Phase 2 might have bugs
- Data quality might be poor
- Report might not look professional
- Might be slow to load

**Mitigation:**
- Test with 5 real IC consultants before Week 1 launch
- Fix any critical issues now
- Create beautiful sample reports (Plano, TX)
- Ensure sub-2-second load times

**Contingency:**
- If bugs found during demo: Have backup static report ready
- "This is the automated version, here's the manual version too"
- Document all bugs, fix in Week 2

---

### Risk #4: IC Consultants Ask For Customization (40% probability)

**Why it happens:**
- Different states have different requirements
- Different utilities have different standards
- They want their logo/branding on reports

**Mitigation:**
- Have templated responses ready
- "Custom builds available after first 10 customers paying"
- "Let's see if the standard product meets 90% of your needs first"
- "We can white-label it at $50K/year once volume justifies it"

**Contingency:**
- Offer limited customization ($2-5K) as upsell
- Build custom feature in Week 2 based on feedback
- Leverage this for sales credibility
