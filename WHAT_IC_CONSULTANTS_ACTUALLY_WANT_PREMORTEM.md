## 💎 WHAT IC CONSULTANTS ACTUALLY PAY FOR

### TIER 1: MUST-HAVE (They will pay immediately)

#### Feature #1: Accurate Site Research (The Core)

```
What it is:
├─ ZIP code + project type → Electrical code + permit requirements
├─ Specific to that jurisdiction
├─ Based on current regulations (not outdated)
└─ Formatted for presentation to clients

Why they pay:
├─ Saves 15-30 hours per project
├─ Current research manually takes 2-3 days
├─ RegGuard delivers in 5 minutes
├─ Labor savings: 15 hrs × $100/hr = $1,500+

IC Consultant quote:
"I'll pay $1,500 per project just to never manually research codes again.
That single feature is worth it. Everything else is bonus."

Price they'll pay: $1-2K per project
Confidence: 95% (will pay)
```

**Why this works:**
- Core pain point (manual research is tedious)
- Easy to value (measure time saved)
- Competitive advantage (faster than law firms)
- Repeatable (every project needs it)

---

#### Feature #2: Interconnection Cost Estimate (Pre-filing)

```
What it is:
├─ "Based on your project type + utility, here's estimated cost to interconnect"
├─ Typical range: $5K-$500K depending on project
├─ Breakdown: utility fees, engineering, equipment
└─ Probabilistic: "50% likely between $50K-$150K"

Why they pay:
├─ Clients ask: "How much will interconnection cost?"
├─ IC consultants don't know (depends on utility review)
├─ RegGuard gives informed estimate
├─ Client makes budget decision
├─ Saves: Embarrassing moment ("I don't know cost")

IC Consultant quote:
"My clients are financing $50M solar projects.
They need cost estimates upfront.
I'm getting killed because I can't tell them interconnection costs.
I'll pay for accurate estimates."

Price they'll pay: $500-1K per project (add-on to core)
Confidence: 85% (will pay)
```

**Why this works:**
- Solves concrete problem (client asks)
- Hard to replicate (requires domain knowledge)
- High client value ($50M project, $100K cost matters)
- Strategic advantage (can bid projects better)

## 🚨 PREMORTEM: WHY CONSULTANTS UNDERPAY

### Failure Mode #1: They Negotiate You Down (70% probability)

```
You say: "$1.5K per project"

First customer agrees to $1.5K
Second customer: "I'll do $1.2K if you throw in timeline estimates"
Third customer: "Can we do $1K for 5-project bundle?"

By Month 6:
├─ Customer A: $1.5K per project (first to sign)
├─ Customer B: $1.2K per project (negotiated)
├─ Customer C: $800/project in bundle ($4K for 5)
├─ Customer D: Wants $700/project or won't sign
└─ Average: $1.1K per project (not $1.5K)

Your forecast: 5 projects × $1.5K = $7.5K
Reality: 5 projects × $1.1K = $5.5K
Shortfall: 26%
```

### Failure Mode #2: They Build It Themselves (60% probability)

```
After 3 projects with RegGuard, IC consultant realizes:
"I could build this myself.
├─ Firecrawl + ChatGPT: $500 labor
├─ Or hire a junior researcher: $1K/week
├─ At $1.5K per project, it pays for itself after 1-2 projects"

Month 4: They start building internal tool
Month 6: Internal tool launched
Month 7: They no longer need RegGuard

Your recurring revenue: Stopped
```

### Failure Mode #3: They Want Annual Subscription (40% probability)

```
IC Consultant: "Can we do $8K/year unlimited for 5 projects?"

Your thinking: "$1.5K × 5 = $7.5K, so $8K is OK"

But then:
├─ They do 3 projects Year 1 (sign annual anyway)
├─ They do 7 projects Year 2 (pay $8K, should pay $10.5K)
├─ They do 9 projects Year 3 (pay $8K, should pay $13.5K)
└─ Your revenue per project drops from $1.5K → $0.9K

Also: You have to support annual renewal/churn
```

## 💡 WHAT THE PREMORTEM REALLY SAYS

**IC consultants will pay for:**

✅ **Things that save them time today** (Site research, cost estimates)  
✅ **Things that are hard to build themselves** (Complex interconnection logic)  
✅ **Things that solve immediate pain** (Per-project, not annual)

❌ **Things they can do manually** (FERC monitoring, dashboards)  
❌ **Things that require their expertise** (Network effects, success prediction)  
❌ **Things that are too complex to justify** (Annual subscriptions for 3-5 projects)
