# RegGuard: Two Paths to Profitability

## THE CHOICE

You have two execution models. Both can work. Each requires different skills/resources.

## PATH A: PARTNERSHIP MODEL (Original Premortem)

**Core Idea**: IC consultants + utilities distribute RegGuard to contractors

Revenue Flow:
```
IC Consultants
├─ Buy white-label RegGuard from you ($8K per project)
└─ Sell to their clients ($20K per project)

Utilities  
├─ Deploy RegGuard in their portal
└─ Mandate contractors use it

Result: Customers come to RegGuard through partners, not cold sales
```

**Financial Impact**:
- Year 1 Revenue: $2-14M
- Gross Margin: 92-95%
- CAC: $0-500 (partners bring customers)
- Breakeven: Month 6-7
- Funding Needed: $0

**Effort Required**:
- Identify 20-30 IC consulting firms
- Send partnership pitches
- Run 2-3 pilots
- Support partners through ramp-up
- Ongoing: Support + documentation (<20 hours/month)

**Timeline to Revenue**:
- Month 1-2: Partnerships signed
- Month 3-4: Pilots active
- Month 5+: Revenue flowing

**Best For**:
- People with existing industry connections
- Patient capital (willing to wait 5+ months for revenue)
- Relationship builders
- People who understand interconnection industry

**Success Probability**: 50-70% (if you have connections), 20-30% (if starting cold)

---

## PATH B: DIRECT SALES MODEL (Harsh Analysis)

**Core Idea**: Direct cold outreach to contractors + free trial conversion

Revenue Flow:
```
Cold Outreach / Ads
├─ Email, LinkedIn, ads, content
└─ Drive to free trial

Free Trial
├─ Contractors try RegGuard
└─ 3-10% convert to paid

Direct Payment
└─ Contractors pay $15K directly to you
```

**Financial Impact**:
- Year 1 Revenue: $50-200K
- Gross Margin: 85-90% (after marketing CAC + support)
- CAC: $10-20K per customer
- Breakeven: Month 15-18+
- Funding Needed: $500K-1M

**Effort Required**:
- Send 100+ cold emails/month
- Run ads/content marketing ($2-5K/month spend)
- Close sales (5-20 hours/week on calls)
- Support customers
- Hiring: Eventually need sales person ($150K+ salary)

**Timeline to Revenue**:
- Month 1-3: Free trial setup + content
- Month 4-6: First customers (slow)
- Month 7-12: Scaling (still slow without funding)

**Best For**:
- Experienced salespeople
- People with marketing background
- People who can raise funding
- People willing to grind on cold outreach

**Success Probability**: 30-40% (without funding), 70% (with $500K+)

---

## PATH C: HYBRID (RECOMMENDED)

**Core Idea**: Get direct customers first (proof), then activate partnerships

Phase 1 (Weeks 1-8): Direct Sales
- Cold outreach to get first 3-5 customers
- Proof concept works
- Collect testimonials/case studies

Phase 2 (Weeks 9-16): Partnership Outreach
- Use customers as proof points
- "We worked with [Customer]. Here's what happened."
- Approach IC firms + utilities with case studies

Phase 3 (Weeks 17+): Hybrid Scaling
- Both channels revenue generating
- Direct sales continues
- Partnerships scale exponentially

**Financial Impact**:
- Year 1 Revenue: $500K-2M
- Gross Margin: 90%+
- CAC: $3-10K (direct) + $0 (partnerships)
- Breakeven: Month 9-12
- Funding Needed: $0-200K

**Timeline to Revenue**:
- Month 1-3: First direct customers ($30-50K)
- Month 4-6: Partnerships initiated ($50-150K)
- Month 7-12: Both scaling ($300-800K)

**Best For**: Everyone (hedges your bets)
