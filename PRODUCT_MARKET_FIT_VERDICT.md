## 🚨 THE PROBLEM WITH CURRENT POSITIONING

### Assumption We Made:
"Data center developers struggle with site diligence research"

### Reality:
"Data center developers struggle with interconnection queue wait times and FERC reviews (things software can't fix)"

### Why Nobody Will Buy RegGuard for Site Diligence:

1. **Developers already use consultants** ($50-500K budget)
   - They're not looking to replace consultant with software
   - Software can't replicate consultant's judgment, relationships, legal expertise

2. **Price/value mismatch**
   - RegGuard: $15K
   - Value delivered: 1-2 weeks of research (worth $5-10K labor)
   - ROI: Negative or break-even
   - Decision: Hire intern instead

3. **Easy to replicate**
   - Law firm: "We already do this, adding Firecrawl takes 2 weeks"
   - ChatGPT: "Just prompt me for permit requirements, it's free"
   - Consultant: "I can train my staff to use Firecrawl"
   - Result: RegGuard becomes obsolete

4. **No network effects**
   - More users = no additional value
   - No defensibility
   - Commodity feature
