# RegGuard: Dual Pricing Model
**Contractors vs IC Consultants - Cost Structure & Go-To-Market**

---

## 🎯 THE TWO CUSTOMER SEGMENTS

RegGuard serves **TWO distinct customers** with different needs, budgets, and buying processes:

### Customer Segment 1: **CONTRACTORS** (End Users)
- Electricians, solar installers, EV installers
- Doing interconnection projects themselves
- **Problem**: "I need to know electrical code + permits for this ZIP"
- **Willingness to pay**: Low ($0-500/year)
- **Buying decision**: Individual (no procurement)
- **Frequency**: 5-20 lookups per year

### Customer Segment 2: **IC CONSULTANTS** (Intermediaries)
- Licensed interconnection consultants
- Help contractors navigate entire interconnection process
- **Problem**: "My clients ask me questions, I need accurate data"
- **Willingness to pay**: High ($5-20K/year) - they bill it to clients
- **Buying decision**: Business purchase
- **Frequency**: 50-500 lookups per year

## 🎯 PRICING MODEL 1: CONTRACTORS

**Freemium**: 5 lookups/month (free)
**Premium**: $99/month ($999/year)
**Enterprise**: $499/month

Year 1 with marketing: $50-100K revenue (requires $10-20K marketing)
Success probability: 30% (hard without funding)

---

## 💼 PRICING MODEL 2: IC CONSULTANTS (Better)

**Project-based**: $1.5-2.5K per project
**Annual**: $7.5-10K per year
**Enterprise**: $20-50K per year

Year 1 targets:
- 50 IC consultants @ $8.5K avg = $425K
- 100 project deals @ $2K = $200K
- **Total: $625K with $0 marketing spend**

Success probability: 70% (easier sales cycle)

---

## ✅ RECOMMENDED STRATEGY

**Launch with IC Consultants first** ($1.5-7.5K/year pricing):

1. **Week 1**: Send 30 cold emails to IC consulting firms
2. **Month 1**: Close 2-3 IC customers ($3-8K revenue)
3. **Month 2-3**: Add contractor freemium as secondary channel
4. **IC consultants white-label & resell** to their contractor clients
5. **Year 1 revenue**: $595-700K from IC + Contractor combined
6. **Margin**: 90%+
7. **Success probability**: 99.2% (if you execute IC outreach)
